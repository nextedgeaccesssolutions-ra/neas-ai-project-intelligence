# Google Play listing — NEAS Field Access

**App name (30 chars max):** NEAS Field Access

**Short description (80 chars max):**

**Full description (4,000 chars max):**

NEAS Field Access is the field app of NextEdge Access Solutions (NEAS) for rope access, façade cleaning, inspection, waterproofing and maintenance work.

For technicians and inspectors:
• Create a field report in four steps: details, photos, work done, sign and submit.
• Take before, after, during, defect and safety photos with the work area and floor. Each photo keeps its capture time and GPS where the camera recorded it.
• Record activities, materials used, remarks, and signatures from the technician, supervisor and client.
• Works without signal: reports and photos are kept on the phone and sent when you are back online. Each report shows "Pending sync" until the server has it.

For project managers:
• Review, approve or return reports, and share approved reports and photos with the client.
• Track project progress, view every site photo by project, area and floor, and see projects on a map.
• Export professional NEAS-branded PDF reports with before-and-after photos.

For clients:
• See only the reports and photos shared with you, on your own projects.

Sign-in is by username and passcode issued by your NEAS administrator. Accounts are created by NEAS; there is no public sign-up.
